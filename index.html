Callan Howland (he/they)

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Rutgers Center for Cognitive Science, and a member of the Cognition in Action lab.

I defended my dissertation in Philosophy at Rutgers University in Summer 2024 under Elisabeth Camp.

Broadly, I work on how perspectives, and the social positions behind them, impact decision and interaction. I think about this work in three parts:

Language: How speakers and hearers build a shared perspective in conversation, and what social dynamics arise when it breaks down or is violated.

Epistemology: How deference to and trust in marginalized perspectives builds solidarity and shapes collective political action more broadly.

Cognition: How social position and perspective impact core areas of cognition, particularly decision, spatial representation, and agency.
